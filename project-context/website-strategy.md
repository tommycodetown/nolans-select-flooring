# **Website Strategy & Architecture Blueprint**

**Nolan Select Flooring — Session 1**

## **1\. Business Objective**

### **Primary objective**

Generate more **high-end residential flooring work**, ideally through **general contractors and contractors managing premium residential projects**.

The ideal enquiry is a contractor handling a **large, high-value Manhattan renovation** requiring complex or custom hardwood flooring work.

### **Secondary objectives**

The website must remain clearly open to:

* Property managers  
* Commercial clients  
* Direct residential clients  
* Government / public-sector work  
* Smaller flooring jobs

The website should **elevate the work Nolan wants more of without implying that smaller or non-luxury projects are unwelcome**.

### **Why contractors are strategically important**

Contractors are especially valuable because:

* they generally understand construction processes and constraints  
* less time is required explaining fundamentals  
* successful relationships can generate repeat work  
* contractors working regularly on premium properties can become high-value long-term accounts

### **Geographic focus**

**Core market**

* New York City / five boroughs  
* Long Island  
* Westchester  
* Nearby New Jersey

**Wider capability**

* Connecticut  
* farther upstate New York  
* wider New Jersey / tri-state area

### **Conversion model**

No enquiry form.

Primary conversion actions:

**Phone**  
**Email**

Both should receive equal prominence.

---

# **2\. Audience Hierarchy**

## **Primary audience**

### **High-end residential contractors / GCs**

The website should demonstrate:

* ability to execute large and complex flooring projects  
* sophisticated custom flooring capability  
* reliability and operational competence  
* strong workmanship  
* evidence from completed projects  
* ability to solve difficult site conditions and flooring problems

## **Secondary audiences**

### **Property managers**

A historically important customer group.

Relevant work includes:

* apartment turnovers  
* repairs  
* refinishing  
* flooring replacement  
* common-area work

### **High-end homeowners**

Direct residential clients seeking premium flooring installation, refinishing, repair or custom work.

### **Commercial / institutional / public-sector clients**

Nolan is open to additional commercial and government work and should not appear residential-only.

### **General residential homeowners**

Ordinary residential projects should remain clearly welcome.

### **Architects / interior designers**

Do not specifically target them at this stage, but the site should not exclude them and may naturally appeal to them through strong project and custom-work presentation.

---

# **3\. Positioning**

## **Internal strategic positioning**

**An established New York flooring contractor, family-run since 1989, with highly experienced long-standing crews and the capability to execute everything from everyday flooring work to large, technically demanding custom installations.**

This is strategic direction rather than final website copy.

### **Desired perception**

The site should communicate:

* established and dependable  
* highly capable  
* experienced with demanding work  
* comfortable on premium residential projects  
* technically competent  
* able to handle scale  
* strong custom craftsmanship  
* professional enough for contractors and commercial clients  
* still accessible to ordinary residential customers

The brand should feel **premium through evidence and execution**, rather than repeatedly describing itself as “luxury.”

# **6\. Homepage Architecture**

## **1\. Hero**

**Purpose:** establish Nolan immediately as a serious, established New York flooring contractor with premium capability.

No final copy or design treatment is being decided in this session.

## **2\. Positioning**

**Purpose:** establish Nolan's market position, breadth of work and relevance to different client types without presenting a literal “Who We Work With” section.

This should communicate audience breadth naturally rather than functioning as an audience directory.

## **3\. Services**

**Purpose:** introduce the main service offering and route users toward the six dedicated service pages.

## **4\. Custom & Decorative Flooring**

**Purpose:** give disproportionate homepage visibility to the specialist work Nolan particularly wants more of.

This links into the dedicated **Custom & Decorative Flooring** service page.

## **5\. Featured Projects**

**Purpose:** provide strong visual proof of capability.

Priority should be given to:

* premium residential work  
* technically impressive projects  
* intricate/custom flooring  
* visually exceptional completed work

## **6\. Why Nolan / Company Proof**

**Purpose:** combine the strongest trust signals with a light introduction to the company.

Potential proof includes:

* established since 1989  
* family-run  
* John \+ Aidan  
* long-standing crews  
* mostly in-house workforce  
* fully insured  
* ability to handle large projects  
* practical problem-solving capability

The full company story belongs on the About page.

## **7\. Service Area**

**Purpose:** provide clear geographic context and support local relevance without becoming a large homepage section.

Keep compact.

Core message should establish:

* based in Maspeth, Queens  
* NYC  
* Long Island  
* Westchester  
* New Jersey  
* wider tri-state capability

## **8\. Contact**

**Purpose:** make starting a conversation frictionless.

Phone and email receive equal prominence.

No enquiry form.

# **7\. Services Overview Page Architecture**

The Services page must be a substantive page rather than simply six links.

## **Structure**

1. Service introduction / positioning  
2. Six primary service routes  
3. Supporting capabilities  
4. Light audience / project-context messaging  
5. Selected project proof  
6. Phone \+ email CTA

### **Strategic emphasis**

**Custom & Decorative Flooring** should receive stronger visual prominence than the other services because it aligns with Nolan's desired future work.

It remains part of the Services architecture rather than receiving header-level navigation.

---

# **8\. Individual Service Page Architecture**

Each service page should follow a broadly consistent information hierarchy without becoming a rigid template.

## **Core structure**

1. **Hero / service introduction**  
2. **What Nolan handles**  
3. **Relevant capabilities / options**  
4. **Project proof**  
5. **Why Nolan for this work**  
6. **Contact — phone \+ email**

Different services can vary in layout and emphasis.

Examples:

* Custom & Decorative Flooring can be highly visual.  
* Floor Repair can lean more heavily into problems and solutions.  
* LVT / Vinyl may naturally feature more property-management and commercial examples.  
* Hardwood Stairs may focus more strongly on execution details and matching adjoining flooring.

There is **no dedicated “Who this service is for” section**.

Audience relevance should appear naturally where appropriate.

---

# **9\. Projects Architecture**

## **Projects page**

The Projects page functions as Nolan's **master proof library**.

### **Structure**

1. Page introduction  
2. **Curated gallery of approximately 6–10 exceptional images**  
3. Individual project index  
4. Contact CTA

### **Gallery role**

The gallery exists to create immediate visual impact.

It should:

* show only excellent work  
* demonstrate breadth  
* emphasise intricate/high-end capability  
* remain separate from project navigation

It should **not** become a huge unstructured photo dump.

### **Project index**

Individual project cards lead directly to actual project pages.

Examples:

* Upper East Side Herringbone Apartment  
* Downtown Loft with Custom Stairs  
* West Village Refinishing Project

Exact project naming will depend on available content.

## **Individual project pages**

Project pages should exist only where enough material is available to create a meaningful case study.

Potential information:

* neighbourhood / general location  
* property type  
* scope  
* flooring/material  
* services performed  
* custom details  
* technical challenges  
* problem-solving  
* photography  
* related services  
* phone/email CTA

Projects without enough information can remain gallery items rather than becoming thin pages.

### **Portfolio strategy**

The portfolio should prioritise the work Nolan **wants more of**, not simply whichever project type has the most photographs.

# **11\. Contact Page Architecture**

## **Structure**

1. Phone  
2. Email  
3. Based in Maspeth, Queens  
4. Service area  
5. Short invitation to discuss a project

### **Rules**

* Phone and email equally prominent.  
* No enquiry form.  
* No business-hours section required.  
* No customer-facing showroom.  
* No need to publish the street address prominently.  
* Do not explicitly instruct contractors to send “plans/specifications/drawings,” but the page should make detailed project enquiries by email feel natural.

# **12\. Geographic / Local SEO Direction**

Nolan is based in **Maspeth, Queens** and operates as a service-area flooring contractor rather than a showroom business.

The website should consistently establish genuine geographic relevance to:

* Queens / NYC  
* the five boroughs  
* Long Island  
* Westchester  
* New Jersey  
* wider tri-state work where appropriate

Do **not** automatically create large numbers of near-identical location pages.

Dedicated geographic landing pages should only be introduced later where:

* real search demand exists  
* the location is commercially important  
* enough genuinely unique content can support the page

Local SEO strategy will be developed separately during the SEO phase.
